# Ministry Stage

**Sends your stage display to any tablet.**

Local screen mirroring for the preacher's stage display, plus a congregation
words page for the morning the projector dies. Everything stays on your own
wifi: nothing is sent to the internet, and there is no account to make.

## Download

**[Download Ministry Stage for macOS](../../releases/latest)**

Open the disk image and drag Ministry Stage into your Applications folder. It is
signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with no warnings.

## What you need

- An **Apple Silicon** Mac (M-series or A18). Intel Macs cannot run it.
- **macOS 12** or later.
- **ProPresenter** on that same Mac.
- The tablets and phones on the **same wifi** as the Mac. Nothing works over
  mobile data, by design.

## Setting it up

The full guide is inside the app: **Help → Ministry Stage guide**. It covers
pointing ProPresenter at the stage screen, setting up a tablet, reserving the
Mac's address on your router, and a Sunday morning checklist.

First run, in short:

1. Drag the app into Applications and open it.
2. Allow **Screen Recording** when asked, then quit and open it again. macOS
   only applies that on a fresh start.
3. Open the address the app shows on your tablet, and tap **Allow** at the desk
   when it appears.
4. Set the tablet's **Auto-Lock to Never**, or it will go dark mid-sermon.

## A word of warning

This is a young app, written for one church and shared in the hope it helps
yours. Please try the words page with ProPresenter open **before** you rely on
it in a service.
