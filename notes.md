**Sends your stage display to any tablet.**

Local screen mirroring for the preacher's stage display, plus a congregation words page for the morning the projector dies. Everything stays on your own wifi.

### What's in this version

- Mirrors a display to approved tablets over your local network. Approve a tablet once and it reconnects on its own.
- A built-in stage screen, so ProPresenter has somewhere to send its stage output without a second monitor.
- A congregation words page at `/words` for a couple of hundred phones, if the projector fails.
- Slide control from the tablet, including a Bluetooth clicker paired to it.
- A menu bar icon for status and quick settings.

### What you need

- An **Apple Silicon** Mac (M-series or A18). Intel Macs cannot run it.
- **macOS 12** or later.
- **ProPresenter** on the same Mac, and everything on one wifi.

### Installing

Open the disk image and drag Ministry Stage into Applications. It is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with no warnings. Allow **Screen Recording** when asked, then quit and open it again: macOS only applies that on a fresh start.

The full guide is inside the app, under **Help → Ministry Stage guide**.

### A word of warning

This is a young app, written for one church and shared in the hope it helps yours. Please try the words page with ProPresenter open **before** you rely on it in a service.
